About

From the front line to running the office.

I'm an operations leader in financial services, based in Kuala Lumpur, working in English, Chinese and Bahasa Malaysia. I started in customer service, moved into payments, KYC and fraud prevention, and today run the Malaysia office of EBC Financial Group.

I write the policies we work by, own the office budget, lead payment integration projects end to end, and look after the people side through HR, learning and admin.

What I enjoy most is turning messy processes into simple ones that people actually follow, and growing the next layer of leaders.
